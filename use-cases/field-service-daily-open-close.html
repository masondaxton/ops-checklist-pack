Open printable checklist

Problem this targets

Generic “ops templates” get ignored. Field crews need a short open/close list that fits a clipboard or phone: vehicle, parts, jobs, cash/card, and end-of-day notes.

How to use today

  1. Print or save Daily open / close.
  2. Strike anything that does not apply to your trade; keep ≤12 checks.
  3. Run it for 5 consecutive workdays before asking the crew what to change.
  4. If onboarding new techs is the real pain, pair with Client onboarding.
